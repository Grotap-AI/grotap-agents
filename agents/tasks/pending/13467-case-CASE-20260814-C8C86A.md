## Requirements
[scan-m] SMLLRP2: Scan M Setup auto-connect never settles - the real cause is auto-connect's own probe sockets left ESTABLISHED on the reader (P2)

SUPERSEDES CASE-20260730-LLRPAC AND ITS SUBTASKS. Those were scoped to a
hypothesis that has since been DISPROVEN, so they are being closed rather than
resumed - re-running them would re-investigate the wrong thing.

WHAT THE OLD CASES ASSUMED (all wrong - do not re-test these):
  - "bindProcessToEthernet() timing / eth0 binding race" (CASE-20260730-02AB5F)
  - "daemon restart is app-caused vs reader firmware" (CASE-20260730-612658)
  - "autoConnectBusyRef / selected:false" - also wrong.

THE ACTUAL CAUSE, ESTABLISHED SINCE:
Auto-connect opens PROBE sockets to the CS463 to discover and verify the reader,
and does not close them. They stay ESTABLISHED on the READER side. The CS463 has
a hard cap on concurrent LLRP connections, so once the leaked probes fill it the
real session can never be established, and the reader appears to restart or to
never settle. The leak is in our probe path - not reader firmware, not the
daemon lifecycle.

SCOPE:
1. Find every socket auto-connect opens purely to probe or discover the reader
   and guarantee it is closed on EVERY path - success, failure, timeout, cancel,
   component unmount. A probe that returns early must still close its socket.
2. Bound concurrent probes. Never open a new probe while one is still
   outstanding for the same reader.
3. Prove it AT THE READER, not in the app: connect over serial (COM7, 115200,
   root/csl - the only way out of an LLRP-wedged state) and show the
   established-connection count returns to baseline after a full
   connect / disconnect / reconnect cycle. An app-side assertion is not proof.
4. Regression test that fails against today's code: simulate N connect attempts
   and assert the reader-side connection count does not grow.

CONTEXT: the CS463 itself was proven working on real hardware on 2026-08-09, so
this is a client-side leak, not a hardware or firmware fault.
