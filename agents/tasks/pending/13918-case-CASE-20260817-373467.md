# Task: Bug: Platform — Hover tooltip on the app icon grid is clipped off the side of the screen near th

## Context
No additional context provided.

## Requirements
Hover tooltip on the app icon grid is clipped off the side of the screen near the viewport edge.

REPORTED BY the owner on 2026-08-17, answering the rejection of ticket #71 / case
0d360d20-668f-43a7-b4c2-05f7f5db860b ("Sub menu does not align properly under icon"):
"when you hover over a platform icon on platform login screen, if you are near the edge
of the screen - the hover over write up is cut off the side of the screen. Test it
yourself and fix."

WHY THE PREVIOUS FIX DID NOT CLOSE IT: commit 5529b6ee aligned the tooltip UNDER the app
icon (horizontal centering relative to the icon). That is correct in the middle of the
grid, but centering on an icon in the last column pushes half the tooltip past the right
viewport edge, so it is clipped. Alignment and clipping are two different defects; only
the first was fixed.

SCOPE: the app icon grid on the landing / My Apps and My Beta surfaces (frontend, the
hover tooltip rendered under each app icon).

ACCEPTANCE:
- The tooltip stays fully inside the viewport for every icon in the grid, including the
  first and last column and the last row, at 1280x800, 1440x900 and 1920x1080.
- When the icon is near an edge, the tooltip shifts along that axis (or flips side)
  instead of being centered and clipped; it still points at / sits under its own icon.
- Verify with Playwright by measuring the tooltip's bounding box against the viewport
  (getBoundingClientRect), not by asserting the element exists — a clipped tooltip is
  still present in the DOM and still passes a text assertion.
- Screenshot proof goes through the screenshot-verifier agent, one cropped shot per
  edge case.
